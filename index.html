Privacy Policy

1. Overview

ScheduleApp is a schedule and to-do management app for families, offering schedule creation, due-time reminders, family-group sharing, and home-screen widgets / Live Activities. It is developed by an independent developer (Frank Zhou) and has no self-hosted server and no backend database. This policy explains how we handle — in fact, do not handle — your information.

2. What We Collect: Nothing

ScheduleApp does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No sign-up, no login. The app has no account system. We do not request or receive your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos or any identifier. Family members are told apart by an anonymous iCloud record name provided by Apple; it contains no name, email or phone number.
  • Your data stays in your own iCloud. Schedules, todos and reminder settings you create are stored in the private iCloud database of your own Apple ID (Apple CloudKit, container iCloud.com.frankzhou.ScheduleApp). The data belongs to you and is held by Apple; the developer cannot access, read or export it.
  • Family-group sharing. Built on Apple CloudKit sharing (CKShare). Schedules you create stay in your iCloud private database; only when you publish a schedule to the family group does it enter the iCloud shared database provided by Apple, visible solely to the family members you invited. Sharing is entirely under your control and can be stopped at any time.

Device capabilities and system permissions:

  • Notifications — used solely for local due-time alerts (schedule/todo alarms) that you set yourself; no advertising or marketing content. Remote (silent) notifications are used only as a trigger for iCloud synchronisation; they never display a visible alert and never carry schedule content.
  • Widgets & Live Activities — widget and Lock Screen data is passed between the app and its widgets through the on-device App Group container. It never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any server.
  • Permissions we never request — no location, contacts, camera, microphone, photo library, or App Tracking authorisation. The app declares no other permissions.

3. Third Parties, Tracking and Advertising: None

  • No third-party code at all — the project depends on no third-party SDK: no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting, attribution or push-marketing component.
  • No tracking — no IDFA, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, no tracking cookies, device fingerprinting or local identifiers.
  • We never sell, share or trade user data, and we do not engage in any form of data monetisation. No data is used for advertising or user profiling.
  • Every network request goes to Apple services — apart from loading this policy page, all network traffic is iCloud/CloudKit synchronisation, governed by Apple's privacy policy. This page is hosted on a third-party static host (GitHub Pages); loading it is the app's only network request to a non-Apple service, and it carries no account or device identifier.
  • We have declared to the App Store, in the app's privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), that the app does not track users and uses no tracking domains.

4. Children's Privacy

  • ScheduleApp is a general-audience family tool. It is not directed at children and is not marketed to children under any specific age.
  • The app collects no one's personal information — including children's. Schedules and todos a child creates are stored only in that child's (or the parent's) own iCloud account; the developer has no access.
  • If a child Apple ID is created and managed by a parent through Apple Family Sharing, that account follows Apple's rules; sharing remains under the parent's control and can be stopped at any time.
  • If you believe we have unknowingly collected a minor's information (which the app's architecture makes impossible), email your.scheduleapp@outlook.com and we will delete it immediately.

5. Data Storage, Security and Deletion

  • All data resides in the iCloud account of your Apple ID, protected by Apple's encryption and access controls. We keep no server-side copy. (For Apple IDs in mainland China, iCloud data is operated by GCBD, Apple's cloud operator in China, under Apple's policies.)
  • Deleting the app does not automatically remove iCloud data (so you can restore on a new device). To erase everything: on iPhone/iPad go to Settings → your Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → ScheduleApp → Delete Data; or delete the schedules/todos and the family group inside the app; or email your.scheduleapp@outlook.com for deletion assistance.
  • A small number of interface preferences (language, display settings) are stored locally on the device (system UserDefaults). They never leave your device and are not synced to iCloud.
  • Local-first: even without iCloud or a network connection, the app keeps working offline and local reminders still fire.
  • Please keep your Apple ID and device passcode secure — they are the primary protection for this data.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Effective Date" shown at the top. Material changes (for example, introducing any new data collection) will also be announced prominently inside the app. Please review this page periodically for the latest version.

7. Contact Us

For any question, complaint or deletion request regarding this policy or your data, email your.scheduleapp@outlook.com. We normally reply within 3 business days.

Support & FAQ

Will I lose my data?

No. Data lives in your iCloud and follows your Apple account. Deleting the app does not delete your iCloud data.

Switching to a new phone?

Sign in with the same iCloud account on the new device and enable iCloud sync for this app — your data appears automatically.

What can family members see?

Only the schedules you publish to the family group. Unpublished schedules stay in your iCloud private database and are invisible to others.

Can the developer read my schedules?

No. Data is stored in your iCloud; the developer has no access channel.

Reminders are not firing?

Check the app's notification permission under Settings → Notifications and that reminders are enabled in the app. Without iCloud the app runs in standalone mode and local reminders still work.
